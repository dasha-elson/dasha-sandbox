# BRD title

## Executive summary
Summarize the opportunity, why it matters, and the proposed direction.

## Background and context
Explain the current state, relevant history, and important context.

## Business opportunity / problem
Describe the business problem or opportunity this document addresses.

## Why now
Explain why this should be worked on now rather than later.

## Stakeholders
List the key teams, roles, or groups involved.

## Approach
Describe the proposed approach at a high level.

## High-level solution summary
Summarize the expected solution without going into detailed product requirements.

## Success metrics
Define how success will be measured.

## Risks and assumptions
List key risks, open assumptions, and important dependencies.

## GTM approach
Summarize launch, enablement, communication, or rollout considerations.

## Open questions
Capture the main unresolved questions that still need answers.
