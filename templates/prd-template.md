# PRD title

## Executive summary
Summarize the feature or deliverable, the problem it solves, and the proposed direction.

## Business value summary
Explain why this matters for customers and for Contentful.

## Detailed solution description
Describe the solution at a high level before getting into detailed requirements.

## Functional requirements
List what the product must do.

## Non-functional requirements
List important constraints such as performance, scalability, reliability, or security.

## Key delivery milestones
Outline the major milestones for delivery.

## Dependencies
List teams, systems, approvals, or projects this work depends on.

## T-shirt sizing
Add a rough size estimate such as S, M, L, or XL.

## How will we measure success?
Define the main success metrics.

## Risks and mitigations
List the biggest delivery or product risks and how they will be handled.

## GTM approach
Summarize any launch, enablement, communication, or rollout considerations.

## FAQ
Capture key questions and answers that stakeholders may ask.

## Changelog
Track major updates to the PRD over time.
