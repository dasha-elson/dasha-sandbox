template for turning repeated patterns into a product signal

# Signal title

## What the problem is
Describe the repeated product problem or pattern clearly.

## Who is affected
List the users, customers, or teams affected.

## Evidence
List the supporting inputs, examples, or references.

## Impact
Explain why this matters and what happens if it is not addressed.

## Root cause
State the likely underlying reason for the problem.

## Current workaround
Describe what people do today to get around the issue.

## Recommendation
Suggest the next step, investigation, or product direction.

## Related artifacts
Link to summaries, BRDs, PRDs, GTM inputs, or other connected documents.
