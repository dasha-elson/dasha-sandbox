pm-ai

This repo is my working space for PM AI workflows, prompts, templates, and product signals.

What this repo is for

This repo helps me:





collect raw product inputs



turn them into summaries and signals



convert validated signals into BRDs, PRD inputs, GTM inputs, and decision-ready documents

Folder overview

0_inputs

Raw source material.

This includes customer call notes, stakeholder notes, sales and SE feedback, Slack threads, support signals, and other early inputs.

1_working

Draft and in-progress analysis.

This includes summaries, clusters, draft signals, draft BRDs, and rough working documents that are not final yet.

2_published

Validated and reusable outputs.

This includes validated signals, BRDs, PRD inputs, GTM inputs, current-state docs, and decision memos.

Weekly workflow





Add new raw notes and source material to 0_inputs



Turn the most important inputs into summaries and draft signals in 1_working



Promote the strongest outputs into validated documents in 2_published
