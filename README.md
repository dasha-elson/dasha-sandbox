# PM Workbench and Artifacts

This repo is my PM workbench for AI-assisted product work.

It is where I collect product inputs, turn them into summaries and signals, and convert the strongest signals into BRDs, PRD inputs, GTM inputs, and decision-ready documents.

## What this repo is for

This repo helps me:
- collect raw product inputs from customer, stakeholder, and internal sources
- synthesize recurring themes into product signals
- turn validated signals into structured product documents
- build a reusable system for PM thinking, writing, and decision support

## Focus areas

This repo is currently focused on:
- Licenses
- Monetization infrastructure, including pricing and packaging
- Enterprise trials and POCs
- Spaces and space management

## How I use this repo

I use this repo as a working system for:
- customer insights and product signals
- PM writing and drafting
- BRD and PRD development
- stakeholder synthesis and decision support

The goal is not to store everything. The goal is to make the most important inputs easier to reuse, compare, and turn into action.

## Folder overview

### 0_inputs

Raw source material.

This includes customer notes, stakeholder notes, sales and SE feedback, support inputs, Slack threads, and other early signals.

### 1_working

Draft and in-progress analysis.

This includes summaries, clusters, draft signals, draft BRDs, draft PRD inputs, and rough working documents that are not final yet.

### 2_published

Validated and reusable outputs.

This includes validated signals, BRDs, PRD inputs, GTM inputs, current-state documents, and decision memos.

### templates

Reusable document structures.

This includes templates for summaries, signals, BRDs, PRDs, GTM inputs, and other repeatable PM artifacts.

### prompts

Reusable AI prompts.

This includes prompts for summarizing inputs, clustering themes, drafting signals, challenging assumptions, and converting signals into documents.

### workflows

Repeatable process guides.

This includes step-by-step workflows such as input-to-summary, summary-to-signal, signal-to-BRD, signal-to-PRD, and signal-to-GTM.

## Core workflow

The main workflow in this repo is:
- collect raw inputs
- summarize and cluster them
- identify repeated patterns
- draft signals
- validate the strongest signals
- convert validated signals into BRDs, PRD inputs, GTM inputs, or decision memos

## Working principles
- start from evidence, not assumptions
- keep raw, working, and published material separate
- treat signals as draft until they are repeated and validated
- use templates so outputs stay comparable over time
- optimize for reuse, clarity, and decision support
